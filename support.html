Oathbound Support

Write to oathboundgymapp@gmail.com. Please include the Support ID from the bottom of Settings (tap it to copy). It identifies this install of the app, not you.

Subscription (Valhalla)

What are the plans? Yearly, with a 14-day free trial, or Monthly, with no trial. Prices are shown in the app and on the App Store in your currency.

How do I start the free trial? On The Oath, keep Yearly selected and tap the trial button. Nothing is charged during the trial. A reminder comes two days before it ends.

How do I cancel? Subscriptions are managed by Apple. Open the Settings app › your name › Subscriptions › Oathbound › Cancel Subscription. Cancel at least 24 hours before the trial or the current period ends. A paid period stays open until it ends; Apple’s page explains what happens when a free trial is cancelled: https://support.apple.com/en-us/118428 Your record is never locked or deleted when a subscription ends.

I paid, but Valhalla is not open. In Settings, tap the Valhalla row, or tap Restore purchases on The Oath. Use the Apple Account you subscribed with. If it still does not open, write to us with your Support ID.

I changed phones. Sign in with the same Apple Account, then restore as above. Your record comes back through iCloud (see below).

I have an offer code. Settings › Valhalla › Redeem a code.

Your record and iCloud

Where is my data? On your iPhone and, if iCloud is on, in your own private iCloud. There is no Oathbound account and no Oathbound server, and the developer cannot see your record.

How does iCloud sync work? If iCloud is on for Oathbound (Settings app › your name › iCloud), your record is kept in your private iCloud. It appears on another iPhone signed in to the same Apple Account. If iCloud is off, the record stays only on this iPhone, and deleting the app deletes it — export it first.

Can I take my data out? Settings › Your record › Export gives CSV and JSON files, always free.

Can I bring my history from Strong or Hevy? Yes. Export a CSV from that app, then use Settings › Your record › Import and choose the file. - Movements are matched to Oathbound’s list. Any that need a look can be reviewed later from the same place. - Cardio and Olympic lifts are kept as your own movements.

How do I delete everything? Settings › Your record › Delete everything. It asks twice, then removes your record from this iPhone and from your iCloud. To delete the usage data linked to your Support ID, write to us with that ID (see the Privacy Policy).

I entered a number by mistake. Open the session, tap Edit, fix it, and seal it again. Totals, lines and marks are counted again; chapters already written stay as written.

Privacy

What does the app send? Usage events, masked screen recordings and crash reports, all linked to a random app identifier (not your name or Apple Account). Firebase also works out an approximate location from the IP address; the app never asks for your location. Purchases are checked through RevenueCat. Details are in the Privacy Policy: https://canilbey.github.io/oathbound/privacy.html

How do I turn analytics off? Settings › You › Share usage data. Off stops usage events, screen recordings and crash reporting.

Logging

Same weight and reps as last time? One tap on the ✓ in the set’s row.

Rest timer: A Rest chip on each set starts it. Auto rest can be turned on for a single movement. Default lengths are in Settings › Recover.

Apple Health: Settings › Apple Health writes each sealed session as a workout. Oathbound never reads Health data.

Notifications: Each kind can be turned off in Settings › Notifications: rest over, week closing, chapter written, echoes, training day reminders, trial ending.

Animals and the weekly line

What does my animal change? The voice your saga is told in, and four of your twelve badges. It does not change your training. Change it in Settings › Path; badges you earned stay.

What decides my weekly line? Your own last four weeks. The first line comes from the number of days you chose at the start. How much heavy sets and working sets weigh is set in Settings › Emphasis, from the next week.
